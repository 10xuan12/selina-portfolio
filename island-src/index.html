How to explore

No set order, no fixed route.
Find a glowing spot, find a story.

Look around
Drag with the mouse or one finger to rotate 360°.
Zoom
Scroll or pinch to zoom in and out.
Walk
Tap the ground, or use WASD / arrow keys. On a phone, hold the arrows at the bottom left.
Find stories
Step into a glowing circle to open a camp. Tapping a camp name or a map dot walks you there.
Switch scenery
