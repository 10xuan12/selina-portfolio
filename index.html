02 — Projects

A few things I've built.

Transportation Company

Sompisi Transport

A family-run private driving service in Mbabane, Eswatini, competing on something a ride-hailing app can't offer: you always know who's driving. I built the site around that — real names instead of a fleet, and a booking flow that skips the backend entirely. Fill in trip details, and it opens WhatsApp with the message pre-written; the owner confirms personally within the hour. No server, no database, no payment processing — just a static site that gets a real conversation started faster.

View live site
University Website

Providence University Graduation Portal

A public university needed one place for graduating students, parents, and faculty to find everything about commencement — ceremony schedules, gown rentals, checkout procedures, honor rolls. I planned, built, and launched the entire site solo, organizing content for three different audiences with different needs and urgency, all in one clear structure.

View live site
Travel Expense App

TripTally

A mobile-first PWA that logs a trip expense — currency conversion included — in under 10 seconds, even offline. Every exchange rate is locked at the moment of entry, so historical records stay accurate regardless of later rate changes. Built solo with Nuxt 3, Vue 3, and TypeScript, running entirely on local storage — no backend, no login. Currently being stress-tested on a real trip to Thailand this October.
