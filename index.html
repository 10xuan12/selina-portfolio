01 About

Hi, I'm Selina.

I studied Information Management for four years. What I actually learned: most "tech problems" aren't tech problems — they're nobody-wrote-down-who's-supposed-to-fix-this problems.

I build frontend, backend, mobile apps, and lately AI-assisted workflows. My last real client was in Eswatini, 8 hours behind Taiwan — we never once had a call, just WhatsApp and a lot of async trust. That taught me more about "the work" than any class did.

I ask annoying questions before I write a single line of code: who's actually using this, where do they give up, what are we quietly assuming. The coding part comes last. It's the easy part.
